THE BIG PICTURE

What is global warming?

Global warming is the long-term rise in Earth's average surface temperature, driven mainly by human activities that increase heat-trapping greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

🌡️

A warming world

Earth's climate is getting warmer, affecting weather patterns, oceans, ice, ecosystems and communities.

🏭

Human influence

Burning coal, oil and gas, land-use changes and other activities add greenhouse gases to the atmosphere.

🌱

Action matters

Cleaner energy, efficient systems, resilient communities and informed choices can reduce climate risks.

WHY IT HAPPENS

Main causes

Several human activities contribute to the buildup of greenhouse gases.

01

Fossil fuels

Coal, oil and natural gas release carbon dioxide when burned for electricity, heat and transport.

02

Deforestation

Removing forests reduces an important natural carbon sink and can release stored carbon.

03

Industry

Industrial processes can emit carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases directly and through energy use.

04

Agriculture

Farming and livestock can produce methane and nitrous oxide, both powerful greenhouse gases.

WHAT CHANGES

Impacts on our world

Warming affects interconnected natural and human systems.

Rising seas

Sea-level rise threatens coastal ecosystems, infrastructure and communities.

Extreme heat

Hotter conditions can increase risks to health, agriculture and ecosystems.

Melting ice

Glaciers and ice sheets are losing mass, contributing to sea-level rise.

Ecosystem stress

Plants and animals may face changing habitats, seasons and food availability.

WHAT WE CAN DO

Solutions start with action.

There is no single solution. Progress comes from combining clean energy, efficiency, responsible land use, innovation and strong climate policies.

03Reduce wasteBuy thoughtfully, reuse items and recycle where systems exist.
04Protect natureSupport forests, wetlands and other ecosystems that store carbon.
